# 🪦 Find a Grave Workstation — A Free Tool for Family Researchers!

Hey everyone! 👋

I wanted to share a free, open-source tool I've been working on for family historians and WikiTree researchers called **Find a Grave Workstation**.

Instead of clicking through Find a Grave one memorial at a time, this tool lets you download records for a cemetery to your computer, automatically link family members, and explore your research offline.

---

### ✨ What It Does:

- 📊 **Cemetery & Family Collector**: Enter a cemetery ID to save memorials and automatically gather connected parents, spouses, children, and siblings.
- 🌳 **Interactive Family Trees**: View interactive pedigree charts for anyone in your database and export `.ged` files to Ancestry, Gramps, or FamilySearch.
- 🗺️ **Satellite Cemetery Maps**: See grave plot locations on satellite maps and draw color-coded lines connecting relatives buried in different cemeteries across state lines.
- 📈 **Stats & Anomaly Checker**: View surname/lifespan statistics and automatically flag date mistakes (like a child born before a parent).
